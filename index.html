Our Heritage

One Pit, Three Generations

In 1952, Shaikh Abdul Ghaffar set up a single coal pit in Karachi and started selling kababs the way his family had always made them — hand-minced, heavily spiced, and finished over real fire.

His Fry Kabab — crisped in desi ghee — became the talk of the neighbourhood. Then came the Special Nihari, simmered overnight until the meat surrenders. Seventy-four years later, the recipes haven't changed, and neither has the fire.

What started as one man's pit is now Karachi's kabab institution — the taste families pass down like an heirloom.
